Privacy

Last updated {{updated}}

{{name}} is a small hobby project: you chat with four ghost characters. This page says what happens to your data. It is written in plain words, not as legal advice.

What we handle

DataWhyWhere it lives
Your chat messages and the repliesTo answer you Sent to our server, then to Google's Gemini service to write the reply. Saved chats are also kept in your own browser.
A request log: time, which page or address was requested, your browser type, status, character, your IP address and country, your message and the reply To fix problems, stop abuse and watch costs A logging service (Axiom). Kept for 30 days, then deleted.
Saved chat historySo your chat is still there when you come back Only in your browser (local storage). The History panel's Clear button deletes it.
Sign in with Google: your Google account id, email address, name and the link to your profile photo (the photo itself stays at Google) To recognise you and save your game progress Our database, until you delete your account.
Game progress (scores, levels, items), when games are availableTo save your progress Your browser, and our database if you sign in.

Who else sees it

  • Google (Gemini) receives your message so it can write a reply. Please don't type anything you want to keep private.
  • Cloudflare runs our server and DNS, and GitHub hosts this website. They see your IP address when you connect, as any website host does.
  • Axiom stores the request log described above.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't use it for advertising.

Sign in with Google

If you sign in, we receive your Google account id, email address, name and the link to your profile photo from Google. We show your name and photo to you on your Me page. We use them only to log you in and to save your progress. We don't read your Gmail, Drive or contacts, and we never ask for those permissions.

Cookies

We use browser storage for your saved chats and settings, and, once you sign in, one cookie that keeps you signed in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

  • Delete your saved chats any time with Clear in the History panel, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete the data we hold about you, including log lines that contain your IP address.
  • When accounts are available, deleting your account removes your profile and progress.
